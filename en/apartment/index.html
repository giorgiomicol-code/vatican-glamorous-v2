Vatican Glamorous

A contemporary loft near the Vatican

An exclusive-use holiday home, recently and carefully renovated in a contemporary loft style, ideal for families and friends of up to 4.

The name “Passeggiata del Gelsomino” (Jasmine Walk) recalls the nearby, evocative avenue that leads to Vatican City.

We are so close to the centre that you will want to walk everywhere.
