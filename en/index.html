Authentic spaces.
Unforgettable stays.

Ideal for families, friends, pilgrims, smart-working, photo shoots and filming.

Vatican Glamorous is an exclusive-use holiday home, renovated in a contemporary loft style, on the fifth floor (with lift) of a typical period building about 200 metres from St. Peter’s Basilica.

It comfortably sleeps up to 4 guests: a bedroom with a king-size bed, a comfortable king-size sofa bed in the living area, a full kitchen, a bathroom and two balconies with a table for breakfast outdoors. Free parking is right below the building.
